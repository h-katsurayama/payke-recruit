What is Payke?

The app changing how tourists shop in Japan

Payke is a shopping support app for international visitors to Japan. Simply scan a product barcode to see multilingual product information — ingredients, usage, reviews, and coupons.

Supporting English, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, and Japanese, we provide everything shoppers need.

As a startup from Okinawa, our mission is to "Raise global consumption", connecting Japanese products with consumers worldwide.
